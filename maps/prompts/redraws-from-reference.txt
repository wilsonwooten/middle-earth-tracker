=== 1. Beleriand (Silmarillion fold-out) ===
The attached photo is a two-page book map of Beleriand, photographed across the gutter. Redraw it as ONE seamless hand-painted illustrated fantasy atlas map, landscape 16:9, at the highest resolution available (4K if possible). Join the two pages into a single continuous map and ignore the page edge, the gutter shadow, and the title box. Keep every coastline, river, mountain range, forest and label in exactly the same position as the reference, and keep all the labels spelled as in the reference in a clean serif font. Style: painted terrain, drawn mountains and forests, deep blue-green sea, green and gold lowlands, dark green forests, grey-white mountains, red-black for the mountains along the top edge, muted parchment feel, like a professional fantasy atlas page. No border, no compass, no extra text.
